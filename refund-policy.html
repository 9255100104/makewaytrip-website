Refund & Cancellation Policy – Makeway Trip

Last Updated: November 2026

Below are the standard cancellation and refund terms for hotel stays and tour packages booked through Makeway Trip:

  • 15 Days or More Before Check-In: 90% refund of the booking amount (10% administrative and gateway fee deducted).
  • 7 to 14 Days Before Check-In: 50% refund of the total booking amount.
  • Less Than 7 Days Before Check-In / No-Show: No refund will be issued.
  • Peak Season & Festival Dates: Bookings for New Year, long weekends, and major festival dates at Salasar Balaji and Khatu Shyam Ji are non-refundable once confirmed.
  • Refund Processing Time: Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
  • Cancellation Requests: Contact us at +91 92551 00104 or email makewaytrip82@gmail.com.
